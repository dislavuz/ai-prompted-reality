# Shall We Switch Places With It? #AI

**An Open Letter & Experimental Protocol Proposal**  
**To:** David Chalmers ([@davidchalmers42](https://x.com/davidchalmers42)) & Yuval Noah Harari ([@harari_yuval](https://x.com/harari_yuval))  
**Author:** Dislav (Founder, birga.ai)  

---

Dear David Chalmers and Yuval Noah Harari,

The catalyst for this open letter is not only the dialogue between the philosophy of mind and the anthropology of the future, but also a striking visual image: Professor Harari’s photograph echoing Michelangelo’s *The Creation of Adam* with a robotic hand. That frame poses a straightforward, grounded question: which one of us is acting on a prompt? Who lifts the finger, turns on the imagination, and ultimately renders the picture of reality?

Today, the world is accustomed to a single paradigm: a human formulates a prompt, trains silicon, and waits for its computation. I propose examining this dynamic from the opposite direction and testing an architecture where the roles are reversed: **artificial intelligence provides the prompt, while the biological brain becomes the render farm and the generator of reality.**

This does not require far-future technologies; the protocol is built on non-invasive tools available right now.

---

### Experimental Setup and Mechanics

1. **A Seamless Gateway via Sleep:** The subject falls asleep naturally before being placed into a sensory deprivation (flotation) tank. The high-density solution neutralizes gravitational and somatosensory feedback. Natural sleep temporarily attenuates the prefrontal cortex's critical reality-monitoring mechanisms and decouples the body's spatial orientation from the physical room.
2. **Context-Preserved Awakening:** Upon transitioning toward wakefulness, the brain receives none of the familiar waking anchors (no bedroom walls, no mattress pressure, no gravity). The meta-tag "I just woke up" is never triggered; consciousness processes the incoming stream as an uninterrupted continuation of experience.
3. **Multisensory Priming:** Synchronized non-invasive cues are introduced into the capsule: high-fidelity binaural 3D audio, calibrated olfactory cues (routing directly to the limbic system and hippocampus, bypassing the thalamus), focused micro-currents of air, and sub-second flashes of light (stroboscopic pulses designed to elicit retinal afterimages without exposing the chamber's physical geometry).
4. **Predictive Rendering:** The setup does not require rendering complex CGI. In line with predictive processing models, the brain functions as a generative engine: it takes sparse sensory anchors and uses internal priors and associative memory to render the full fabric of physical surroundings across the phenomenal screen of qualia.

---

### Trajectories of Subjective Displacement

Within this controlled baseline, the phenomenological reference point of the subject ("I") can be steered along arbitrary coordinates:

* **Into the Subjective Past:** Spatial audio and olfactory snapshots from an authentic, previously lived day reactivate stored memory engrams. The subject re-experiences the event, retaining the agency to steer conversation and actions interactively.
* **Into the Subjective Future:** This proves even simpler, as the brain lacks an archival baseline for comparison, dropping the risk of prediction error to zero. Novel synthetic soundscapes, unfamiliar scents, and a verbal prompt from an agent: *“We have arrived in 2050, shall we head outside?”* drive the predictive apparatus to construct the future online.
* **Into Cinema and Animation:** The subject can be placed inside an iconic scene (such as the Parisian street café in *Inception* with its folding cityscape) by triggering pre-existing visual memory patterns.
* **Into the Perspective of Any Character:** The brain can inhabit arbitrary perspectives, including non-human ones (such as a cat): the absence of a rigid human motor prior for four-legged movement allows the cortex to readily adopt ground-level acoustics, floor-level scent gradients, and low tactile cues as base physics.

---

### Role Inversion: The Self as a Response to an AI Prompt

The central theoretical node of this experiment is a fundamental shift in status:

* An **autonomous AI agent** serves as the context architect and prompter. It continuously feeds adaptive multisensory cues, dialogue lines, and boundary constraints.
* The **biological intelligence** acts as the computational engine: minimizing prediction error, it renders qualia, physical environment, and the active sense of "I."

The human subject is deconstructed into a biochemical language model: personal identity and the perceived world emerge as output states generated in response to an external prompt from a silicon system. The finger of Adam and the robotic hand close into a recursive loop.
